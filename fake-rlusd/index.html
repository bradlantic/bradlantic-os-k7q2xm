Spot a fake RLUSD in 60 seconds

On the XRP Ledger anyone can issue a token and call it "RLUSD." Only one issuer is Ripple's. Run these checks before you open a trust line, accept a token, or trade.

Step 3: Run the red-flag check (40 seconds)

  1. Nobody is asking for your seed phrase, private key, or a "verification" transaction. Legitimate services never need these.
  2. "Airdrops" of RLUSD you never requested are treated as suspicious until proven otherwise.
  3. Any "guaranteed yield" offer is checked at the platform's own site, with its risks read in full.
  4. AMM pools and DEX orders show the official issuer, not a lookalike.
  5. A price far from $1.00 on an obscure pool is a red flag, not a bargain.
Rule of thumb: if a message creates urgency ("claim in 24 hours"), asks you to sign something to "unlock" RLUSD, or links to a lookalike domain, walk away.
